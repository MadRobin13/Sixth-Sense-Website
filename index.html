Adaptive Safety for Independent Living

Sixth Sense is an accessibility-focused monitoring system that helps older adults live safely and independently while allowing caregivers to monitor them remotely. It provides fall detection, contextual assistance, and a live video feed — all without requiring seniors to learn new technology.

Why Sixth Sense Works

A passive, always-on system designed to protect independence while keeping families informed — not overwhelmed.

📡 Fall & Distress Detection

Monitors for potential falls, and sudden movements using camera and audio analysis. Alerts caregivers instantly when something may be wrong.

📝 Contextual Assistance

Helps users with mobility or vision challenges by identifying objects and describing surroundings on demand.

♿ Accessibility-First

Designed for older adults: simple, intuitive, and unobtrusive. Technology adapts to the user — never the other way around.

How It Works

Local video and audio analysis combined with an intuitive caregiver app. Sixth Sense supports safety without invasive monitoring.

📷 Vision Processing

A Raspberry Pi processes a live camera feed to detect falls, motion patterns, and environmental context.

🧠 React + TypeScript App

A mobile-focused interface built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Radix UI for caregivers to view live video, check activity, and receive alerts.

📞 Smart Notifications

Alerts caregivers when falls, or unusual inactivity are detected, helping them respond quickly when needed.

The Sixth Sense App

Built for caregivers — not seniors. The app provides a simple, clear dashboard with video feed, motion status, search/describe tools, and real-time alerts.
